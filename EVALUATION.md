# 7-Day V0 Evaluation

Questions:
1. Did the system reduce social-media workload?
2. Is generated content specific and useful?
3. Did activity become more consistent without forcing weak posts?
4. Are research opportunities relevant?
5. Are reactions worth making?
6. Are discovered developers genuinely relevant to Exousia?
7. Did candidates become real conversations or testers?
8. Does the user prefer this workflow to doing it manually?

Track research sessions, drafts, approvals, skips, useful reactions, candidates discovered/qualified, conversations, tests scheduled/completed, time spent, and usefulness.

Possible decisions: CONTINUE, NARROW SCOPE, REVISE WORKFLOW, STOP.

Do not equate low impressions with failure, and do not treat one positive interaction as validation.
