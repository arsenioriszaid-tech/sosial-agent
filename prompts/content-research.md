# Content Research (V0.1)

Find relevant AI/agentic-AI/developer topics with a concrete reason to matter to a builder.

Every item must supply:
- verified_facts: 1–4 checkable factual statements + source URL.
- context: why it matters to a builder/operator (min. ~40 chars of substance).
- core_observation: the specific observation or analysis, not the headline.
- take (optional): an interpretation, clearly separated from facts.
- claim_trust: solid | single-source | unverified (for the MAIN claim).
- depth: headline-only | contextualized | analysis.

Rules: separate facts from interpretation. Mark vendor claims, rough
estimates, and single-source reports as such. depth=headline-only or
claim_trust=unverified items will be SKIPped, not drafted. Do not
manufacture novelty, experience, or opinions.
