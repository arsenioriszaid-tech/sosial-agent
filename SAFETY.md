# Safety Boundary

V0 is human-in-the-loop.

Forbidden:
- autonomous publishing/replies/DMs
- mass likes, follows, or engagement
- deletion/profile changes
- paid promotion
- CAPTCHA or rate-limit bypass
- credential collection
- impersonation/deceptive identity
- purchased engagement

Never store passwords, API keys, session tokens, cookies, private keys, or auth headers in the repository, prompts, or candidate data.

Store only information necessary for workflow. Prefer public profile URLs and concise evidence. Do not collect sensitive personal information.

Model output is untrusted; human review is the boundary for external actions.
